# plan_rolling.md

The rolling plan, ~3-4 GW deep. Opened 20 Nov 2026 at the GW10 sunset (`review_gw10.md`, R17).
The weekly loop consults it (step 6) and amends it (step 10).

## Amending this plan

- Grounds are a closed list: availability, role, set piece, a real club transfer, a change in the live fixture grid. Anything else is logged as "judgment", in that word (R11).
- Each amendment names its evidence with a tag, quotes the line it replaces, and goes in as a dated append below. Never rewrite in place.
- Log it in `ledger.md` prose. `decision_log` is transfer rows only.
- No deadlines are typed here. Read `events[].deadline_time` live (R1).
